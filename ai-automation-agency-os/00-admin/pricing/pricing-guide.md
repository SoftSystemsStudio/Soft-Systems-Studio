## Objection Handling

### "Rosie/Goodcall is only $49/month"

> "Those are self-service templates. You sign up, answer a few questions, and get a generic AI that sounds like everyone else's.
>
> We build a custom voice agent trained specifically on YOUR business — your services, your pricing, your personality. When someone calls, they're talking to YOUR receptionist, not a template.
>
> Plus, we handle everything. You don't have to figure out integrations, write scripts, or troubleshoot issues. We optimize it monthly based on real call data.
>
> For $150 more per month, you get a premium experience that actually represents your business well."

### "Why is there a setup fee?"

> "The setup fee covers the custom work we do for your business:
>
> - Recording and training your custom AI voice
> - Learning your services, pricing, and common questions
> - Integrating with your calendar and CRM
> - Testing and quality assurance
> - Training you on the dashboard
>
> This isn't a template we copy-paste. We build this specifically for you, and it takes 10-15 hours of work.
>
> Unlike agencies charging $5,000-$10,000, our $997 setup is accessible while still being truly custom."

### "I can't afford $200/month"

> "I understand budget is important. Let me ask — how many calls do you miss per month?
>
> If you're like most service businesses, it's 20-30%. If you get 100 calls and miss 25, and your average job is $300... that's $7,500 in lost revenue.
>
> The AI costs $250/month total. It pays for itself if it books you ONE extra job.
>
> We can also start with just the website ($2,500 one-time) and add the AI later when you're ready."

### "Why not just hire someone?"

> "A part-time receptionist costs $1,500-$2,000/month minimum. Full-time is $3,000+. And they can only answer one call at a time, don't work nights/weekends, get sick, take vacations, and eventually quit.
>
> The AI is $250/month, answers unlimited calls simultaneously, works 24/7/365, never calls in sick, and never needs training again.
>
> Plus — the AI texts you a summary of every call. You always know what's happening, even when you're on a job."

### "I want to think about it"

> "Totally understand. Quick question — what's the main thing you're unsure about?
>
> [If price:] We can start smaller with just the website or just the AI.
>
> [If trust:] Would it help to do a quick demo call where you can talk to a sample AI and see how it works?
>
> [If timing:] When would be a better time? I can follow up then."

## Sales Process

### 1. Discovery Call (15-30 min)

- Understand their business and pain points
- Ask about missed calls, current website, lead flow
- Qualify budget ($3,000-$5,000 realistic for them?)

### 2. Demo (if needed)

- Let them call the demo AI receptionist
- Show example website in their industry
- Walk through dashboard

### 3. Proposal (same day)

- Send pricing with recommended package
- Include timeline and payment terms
- Follow up within 24 hours

### 4. Close

- Address objections
- Collect deposit
- Schedule kickoff call
