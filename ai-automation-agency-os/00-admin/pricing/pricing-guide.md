## Objection Handling

**"That's more than I expected"**

> We price based on ROI. Our voice receptionist saves clients $2,000+/month in labor. The setup pays for itself in 3 months.

**"Can you do it cheaper?"**

> We can adjust scope. What's most important to you? We can phase the project to fit your budget.

**"I found someone cheaper on Fiverr"**

> You'll get a template, not a system. Our solutions are production-grade, integrate with your tools, and include ongoing support.

**"Why monthly fees?"**

> AI models cost money to run. Our monthly fee covers hosting, model access, monitoring, and support. It's less than 1 hour of your time per week.
