# Discovery Call Script

**Purpose:** Qualify leads, understand needs, build rapport, and position our solution.

**Duration:** 30 minutes

---

## Before the Call (2 min prep)

- [ ] Review intake form submission
- [ ] Check their website/LinkedIn
- [ ] Note industry and potential pain points
- [ ] Have pricing guide open

---

## Opening (3 min)

### Build Rapport

> "Hi [NAME], thanks for taking the time today. Before we dive in, I'd love to hear a bit about you — how did you hear about us?"

_Listen actively, find common ground_

### Set the Agenda

> "Here's what I'd like to cover in our 30 minutes:
>
> 1. Learn about your business and what you're trying to accomplish
> 2. Share how we might be able to help
> 3. Discuss next steps if it makes sense
>
> Sound good?"

---

## Discovery Questions (15 min)

### About Their Business

> "Tell me about [BUSINESS NAME]. What do you do and who do you serve?"

**Listen for:** Industry, business model, target customer, team size

### Current Situation

> "Walk me through how things work today. For example, when a potential customer reaches out, what happens?"

**Listen for:** Manual processes, bottlenecks, missed opportunities

### Pain Points

> "What's the biggest challenge you're facing right now with [topic from intake form]?"

**Follow-up probes:**

- "How long has this been an issue?"
- "What have you tried so far?"
- "What happens if this doesn't get solved?"

### Goals

> "If we could wave a magic wand, what would the ideal situation look like 6 months from now?"

**Listen for:** Specific outcomes, metrics they care about

### Urgency & Timeline

> "How soon are you looking to get this solved? Is there a specific deadline or event driving this?"

### Decision Process

> "Who else is involved in making this decision? What does your process typically look like for a project like this?"

### Budget

> "Do you have a budget range in mind for this project?"

_If hesitant:_

> "To give you context, projects like yours typically range from $X to $Y depending on scope. Does that align with what you were thinking?"

---

## Present Solution (7 min)

### Transition

> "Based on what you've shared, I think we can definitely help. Let me share how we typically approach this..."

### Position the Solution

> "For [their situation], we'd recommend our [PACKAGE NAME]. Here's what that includes..."

_Tie features to their specific pain points:_

- "You mentioned [PAIN] — our [FEATURE] specifically addresses that by [BENEFIT]"

### Social Proof

> "We recently worked with a [similar industry] client who had the same challenge. After implementing our solution, they [SPECIFIC RESULT]."

### Investment

> "For a project like this, you're looking at approximately $[RANGE] for setup, plus $[RANGE]/month ongoing. The typical ROI we see is [X] — most clients see payback within [TIMEFRAME]."

---

## Handle Objections

### "That's more than I expected"

> "I understand. What were you expecting? ... Let's talk about what's most important to you. We can sometimes phase the project or adjust scope to fit your budget."

### "I need to think about it"

> "Of course. What specifically do you want to think through? I want to make sure you have all the information you need."

### "I'm talking to other providers"

> "That makes sense. What criteria are you using to make your decision? ... What would make us the obvious choice?"

### "Can you send me something?"

> "Absolutely. I'll send you a custom proposal within 48 hours. When would be a good time to reconnect and walk through it together?"

---

## Close & Next Steps (5 min)

### If Qualified & Interested

> "Based on our conversation, it sounds like there's a strong fit. Here's what I'd suggest for next steps:
>
> 1. I'll send you a custom proposal by [DATE]
> 2. Let's schedule a follow-up call for [DATE] to review it together
> 3. If everything looks good, we can get started as soon as [DATE]
>
> Does that work for you?"

### If Needs More Time

> "I completely understand. How about I send over some information and we reconnect in [TIMEFRAME]? What would be the best way to follow up?"

### If Not a Fit

> "I appreciate you taking the time today. Based on what you've shared, I don't think we're the right fit for [REASON]. However, I'd recommend looking into [ALTERNATIVE]. Best of luck with your project!"

---

## Wrap Up

> "Any other questions before we wrap up? ... Great, I'll get that proposal over to you by [DATE]. Looking forward to potentially working together, [NAME]!"

---

## After the Call

- [ ] Send follow-up email within 2 hours
- [ ] Add notes to CRM
- [ ] Create proposal (if qualified)
- [ ] Schedule follow-up
- [ ] Add to nurture sequence (if not ready)

## Qualification Scorecard

| Criteria                  | Score (1-5) |
| ------------------------- | ----------- |
| Clear pain point          |             |
| Budget aligned            |             |
| Timeline urgent           |             |
| Decision maker            |             |
| Good fit for our services |             |
| **Total**                 | /25         |

**Scoring:**

- 20-25: Hot lead — prioritize proposal
- 15-19: Warm — needs nurturing
- <15: Not qualified — add to long-term nurture

---

## Red Flags

🚩 "Can you just do this one small thing?"  
🚩 No clear decision maker  
🚩 Budget way below minimum  
🚩 Wants to start immediately without process  
🚩 Badmouths previous vendors excessively  
🚩 Unclear on what they actually want
