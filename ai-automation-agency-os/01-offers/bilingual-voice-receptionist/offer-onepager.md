# Bilingual Voice Receptionist - Offer One-Pager

## Value Proposition

A 24/7 AI-powered receptionist that handles calls in both English and Spanish, ensuring no lead is missed and every patient feels heard.

## Key Features

- **Bilingual Support:** Seamless switching between English and Spanish.
- **24/7 Availability:** Never miss a call, even after hours.
- **Intelligent Routing:** Directs calls to the right person or department.
- **Appointment Scheduling:** Integrates with calendars to book appointments directly.
- **FAQ Handling:** Answers common questions about services, hours, and location.
- **Call Summaries:** Provides detailed summaries and transcripts of every call.

## Target Audience

- Medical Practices (Midwifery, Dental, General Practice)
- Service Businesses (Plumbing, HVAC, Legal)
- Real Estate Agencies

## Pricing Model

- **Setup Fee:** One-time implementation cost.
- **Monthly Subscription:** Recurring fee for maintenance and platform usage.
- **Usage Fees:** Per-minute billing for voice processing.
